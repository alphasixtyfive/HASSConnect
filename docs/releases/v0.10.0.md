# HASS Connect 0.10.0

You can now open a live Home Assistant camera from Quick Access or from a
Windows notification. If you turn on automatic camera popups, a doorbell or
intercom automation can open the camera as soon as it rings. A snapshot can
show while the live feed connects.

Sound starts on and the microphone starts muted. If your camera supports local
go2rtc talkback, you can hear and speak to visitors. You can also add buttons such as
**Open gate** in the camera tile’s settings. Cover controls ask you to confirm
before sending the command.

If you leave your PC running while you’re away, turn on **Only show while
active** in Notifications. HASS Connect checks the Windows lock state, display
power, and whether you’ve used the keyboard or mouse in the last five minutes.

HASS Connect keeps one running copy per Windows profile. When Windows starts the
app, it opens in the tray.
